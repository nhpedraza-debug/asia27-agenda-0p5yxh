1. Trip concept

A westward round-the-world trip, from warm to cold

A leisure journey across Asia (winter–spring 2027), comfort level, for 3 adults. Eleven countries in 127 days, always moving west on one-way flights: Florida ✈ Honolulu, Southeast Asia in its dry season, Japan and China in spring (Japan for the cherry blossoms), Korea in May, and the close in Uzbekistan and Türkiye once the cold is gone. No round-trip hub.

Climate and holidays dictate the order: Thailand after Chinese New Year (Feb 6), Japan runs Mar 27–Apr 17 to catch the Tokyo and Kyoto blossoms at their peak and close with the late Alpine bloom (Takayama with its Apr 14–15 festival and the Tateyama Kurobe Alpine Route), well before Golden Week; China runs Apr 17–27, between Qingming and May Day, capped at 10 nights so the visa-free transit always works, and Türkiye limited to Istanbul and Cappadocia, as you asked.
